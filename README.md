# FinnG Retire — Projection Calculator

A comprehensive retirement planning tool built for everyone - US citizens, international workers, immigrants, veterans, and anyone planning their financial future.

## What It Does

FinnG Retire runs 500 Monte Carlo simulations to project your retirement outcomes, accounting for real-world risks that most calculators ignore.

**Core Projections**
- Net worth at retirement across best, median, and worst-case scenarios
- Monthly retirement income breakdown (investments + Social Security + pension)
- Mortgage payoff timeline with principal vs interest tracking

**Risk Analysis**
- Fat-tail distributions that model market crashes 2-3x more realistically than standard calculators
- Job loss probability during crashes
- Scenario comparison: with and without crash protection (bucket strategy)
- Probability of hitting your financial targets

**Tax Modeling**
- Federal tax brackets on retirement withdrawals
- Dual state taxation (for those who live in one state and work in another)
- Roth vs traditional account comparison showing after-tax impact

**Healthcare**
- Projected Medicare premiums, supplemental insurance, and out-of-pocket costs
- Lifetime healthcare cost estimate

**For International Workers**
- Social Security quarters tracker with 40-quarter eligibility threshold
- Totalization agreement verification for 30+ countries
- "Leave US" scenario: what happens to your accounts, Social Security, and taxes if you return to your home country
- Currency risk modeling for retiring abroad
- 30% withholding rules on retirement account withdrawals for non-residents

## How to Use

1. Visit the app in your browser
2. Go to the **Inputs** tab and enter your information
3. Click **CALCULATE**
4. Review your results across the Dashboard, SS Tracker, Leave US, and Simulator tabs
5. Adjust inputs and re-calculate to explore different scenarios

## Supported Account Types

- 401(k) / 403(b) with employer match
- Roth IRA
- Traditional IRA
- 457(b)
- HSA (Health Savings Account)
- Taxable brokerage
- Pension

## Privacy

FinnG Retire runs 100% in your browser. No data is collected, stored, or sent anywhere. No account required. No login. No tracking. Your financial information stays on your device.

## Install as an App

- **Android**: Open in Chrome → tap menu → "Install app"
- **iPhone**: Open in Safari → tap Share → "Add to Home Screen"
- **Desktop**: Open in Chrome or Edge → click the install icon in the address bar

Once installed, it works offline and appears on your home screen like a native app.
