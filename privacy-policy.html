FinnG Retire — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 22, 2026

Summary

FinnG Retire does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Period.

How the App Works

FinnG Retire is a retirement projection calculator that runs entirely in your web browser. All calculations — including Monte Carlo simulations, tax projections, mortgage tracking, and Social Security estimates — are performed locally on your device using JavaScript.

Data Collection

We collect no data. Specifically:

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email, phone number, or address. We do not collect financial information such as salary, account balances, investment amounts, or any numbers you enter into the calculator. We do not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, or any form of usage monitoring. We do not use advertising or ad-related tracking of any kind. We do not transmit any data to any server, database, API, or third party.

Data Storage

FinnG Retire does not store your data anywhere. When you close the browser tab, all information you entered is gone. There is no account system, no login, no cloud storage, and no local storage. Every session starts fresh.

Third-Party Services

FinnG Retire does not integrate with any third-party services, APIs, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or data brokers. The app loads one external resource: Google Fonts for typography. Google's font service privacy policy is available at fonts.google.com.

Children's Privacy

FinnG Retire does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13. The app is designed as an educational financial planning tool.

Security

Because no data is collected or transmitted, there is no data to breach. Your financial information exists only in your browser's memory while you are using the app and is discarded when you leave.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change how this app handles data, we will update this policy. Given our commitment to privacy-first design, we do not anticipate collecting user data in the future.

Open Source Verification

The complete source code of FinnG Retire is a single HTML file. You can inspect it yourself to verify that no data is collected or transmitted. Right-click the app and select "View Page Source" in any browser.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the FinnG team through our GitHub repository at github.com/wirdani84/finng-retire.
